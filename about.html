About Pantone3D

Materials for a brighter tomorrow.

Pantone3D is built around a simple idea: to create dependable 3D printing materials for makers, creators, professionals and industries.

Our story

From an idea to a material people trust.

Pantone3D began with a simple belief: the material should never be the thing that holds an idea back.

So we focus on filaments made to behave predictably, with consistent flow, dependable colour and clean results, leaving makers free to concentrate on what they are making.

Values

What drives us.

  • Quality first

    Every material is judged by how dependably it prints, not only by how it looks on the spool.

  • Innovation

    We keep exploring materials, finishes and colours for the work people want to do next.

  • For makers

    Made for the people who prototype, design, teach and build, from the first print onwards.

  • Responsible progress

    Growing with care for the people who use our materials and the places their work lives.
